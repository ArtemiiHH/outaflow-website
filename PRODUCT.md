## Platform

## Stack
React, Vite, React Router, TypeScript, modern CSS. Specified in CODEX-INSTRUCTIONS.md.

## Product Purpose
A minimal clothing storefront for scheduled releases. Customers discover an active drop, select a size, and complete payment on Shopify. Between releases, visitors can opt into email updates.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Shopify owns inventory, pricing, customer consent and hosted checkout. No custom database, accounts, CMS or checkout. Public Storefront credentials may be shipped; Admin credentials must remain server-side. Client scheduling is presentation, not secure purchase eligibility enforcement.
